Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Budget App is a local-first web app. Your entries are stored in your browser localStorage only after you accept cookie/storage consent.

What data we store

  • Income/expense entries you create.
  • Language preference (English/中文).
  • Cookie/storage consent choice.

How data is used

Data is used only to run the app features in your browser. No server-side upload is performed by this project.

Your choices

  • You can decline consent; then entries are not persisted.
  • You can clear browser storage at any time to remove all saved data.

隐私政策（中文）

本应用为本地优先应用。仅在你同意后，才会把账目数据保存到浏览器 localStorage，不会上传到服务器。

你可以随时在浏览器清理本地存储来删除所有数据；若拒绝同意，应用不会持久化保存账目。
